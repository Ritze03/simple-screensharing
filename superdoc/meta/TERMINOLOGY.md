# Terminology

Project-specific vocabulary. When the user uses a term defined here, use the same
meaning.

**If the user uses a non-standard term that is not defined here, ask what they mean by
it before acting on it.** Once its meaning is clear, add it to this file. Keep this
file up to date automatically: whenever the user introduces or clarifies a term, add or
amend the entry here.

## Terms

- **Suspended room** — a joined room whose connection dropped unexpectedly: its `RoomSession` is gone but a `ReconnectState` (`AppState::reconnects`) keeps its handle, last-known roster and what the user had running (watches, our shares) while the client retries. Not the same as a room the user left. See `architecture/implementation-notes.md` (task #36).
- **Connecting vs Reconnecting** (UI) — `ui::ConnState::Connecting` is the *first* connect of a room in flight; `Reconnecting` is only a suspended room (above). Different pill text, same panels. `ui/APP-SHELL.md`.
- **Dynamic / Static** (rate control, historical) — the two Rate-control choices stream settings had from 2026-10-15 (#87; formerly "Efficient" / "Max quality") until the choice was **removed the same day (#88)**: encoding is now always **Dynamic** (VA `rate-control=vbr`, bitrate held near 95 % of the configured value, which is a real ceiling; x264 plain `bitrate=`). **Static** was a constant quantizer (CQP): bitrate followed the content, the configured kbps was NOT a cap, and it overflowed the link and froze streams on motion. `RateControlMode` and the `rate_control` setting are gone; old settings files containing `rate_control = "cqp"|"qvbr"` still load (the key is ignored). `features/stream-control.md` "Rate control".
- **Viewer recovery (L1 / L2 / L3)** — the native viewer's escalation when a watched stream stops decoding well (#101, 2026-11-02): **L1** asks the sharer for a new keyframe by Unsubscribe+Subscribe (the only keyframe request a viewer has), **L2** also resets the decoder pipeline, **L3** reconnects the room because the path is dead. An *episode* is one stretch in which a keyframe is wanted; the *gate* drops deltas only while the decoder has no valid state. `decode/recovery.rs`, `features/video-pipeline.md` "Viewer recovery".
- **NACK** (retransmission, #104, 2026-11-03) — a viewer (native or browser) asking for the media fragments it is missing instead of discarding the frame. Not a control message: short (< 19 byte) datagrams that every older peer drops as undersized. **Hello / HelloAck** is the capability probe (a viewer NACKs only after the relay acked), a **Request** names a frame and a fragment range, a **Miss** tells the viewer the relay cannot supply it. Answers come from the **relay cache** (the datagrams it forwarded in the last 2 s) first, from the sender's **send ring** second; a re-sent fragment carries the **retransmit flag** (media `flags` bit2) so only the viewers that asked get it. While a frame is being repaired the viewer holds later frames of that stream and releases them in order. `protocol-spec.md` §6.6, `features/video-pipeline.md` "Selective retransmission".
- **Loudness levelling** ("Level loudness", #119, 2026-11-03) — the viewer-side automatic gain control on every watched audio stream: a slow AGC toward about -18 dBFS RMS (rises <= 4 dB/s, never in near-silence, falls fast) followed by a zero-attack peak limiter at -1 dBFS that makes a sudden loud sound unable to blast the listener. Per-viewer setting, default on, native `leveller.rs` and browser `docs/js/core/leveller.js`. Not the sender's **Boost** (a manual 50-200 % trim before encoding) and not the per-stream volume slider (which acts after it). `features/audio-sharing.md`.
- **Webcam in picture** (PIP, #137, 2026-11-04) — a Layout-menu option that puts a person's webcam inside their screen-share tile (draggable, resizable, aspect-locked) when one is playing; otherwise the webcam stays a normal tile. Built in both clients (task I8 native `ui/stage/pip.rs`, I10 browser `docs/js/ui/pip.js`), per view and per webcam; no wire field exists for it (the pairing is computed by each viewer: a webcam is the stream labelled `Webcam` of the same sender). See **PIP spot**. `ui/STYLING-GUIDE.md` "HTML replica", `ui/APP-SHELL.md`, `features/website.md`.
- **Stage** — the central area of a joined room and of "Watched streams" (2026-11-04, tasks I1-I10): a black area with the room's streams as tiles, the people strip under them and the **control bar**. Native `client/src/ui/stage/`, browser `docs/js/ui/stage.js`. It replaced the old "On screen" tiles and "Sharing in this room" cards and the bottom action bar. `ui/APP-SHELL.md`, `features/website.md` "The stage".
- **Split / Crop / Floating** — the three layouts of the Stage (Layout menu): **Split** shows every picture whole in an even-ish grid (the row count that shows the most picture area, `geom::best_rows`), **Crop** fills the same cells (picture cropped around its centre, `Fit::Cover`), **Floating** gives each stream a window you move and resize (aspect kept). Not the older `ui::PresentationMode` (render vsync mode, `features/presentation-modes.md`).
- **Resize bar** — the draggable bar between two pictures of a Split or Crop grid (it sits midway between the *pictures*, not on the cell edge); a double-click evens the grid out. `ui/stage/geom.rs` `Bar`, `drag_bar`.
- **Chrome** (stage chrome) — the people strip, control bar and tile overlays that lie over the stage in fullscreen and auto-hide when the pointer rests (`ui/stage/chrome.rs`, browser `chrome.js`). Outside fullscreen the chrome always shows. Not the browser's own chrome.
- **Split button** — a control-bar button with a dropdown arrow cut off by a separator line (Share screen, Share audio, Webcam, Layout, Room settings): the main part acts at once, the arrow opens the menu. A lit one is green (live) or accent (ready). `ui/stage/bar.rs`.
- **Desktop audio** — the audio button's default source (task I7, Linux only): every app's audio streams you hear except those on the **exclusion list**, linked into the share sink by the 1 s poll. The other choice is one named app (`AudioChoice::Named`). `features/audio-sharing.md`.
- **Source group** — the heading an audio source is listed under (Games, Browsers, Media, Voice chat, Devices, Other), derived from the app's name and role by `audio.rs` `classify` (`AudioGroup`).
- **Exclusion list** — `AppSettings::audio_exclusions`: texts; an audio source whose name contains one (ignoring case) stays out of Desktop audio and off every source list. Discord and TeamSpeak are on it by default. Edited in the audio settings menu ("Add" popover: detected sources or typed text).
- **Emoji avatar** — the emoji a person picks as profile picture (Settings > Profile > Avatar; the picker shows 40, but native draws **any** emoji from its embedded Noto Color Emoji font: one grapheme, at most 16 bytes, shaping to a single bitmap glyph, centred on its balance point). Its colour sets the avatar's background; anything else shows as the initial. Carried by the relay in cleartext (`AvatarHello` / `AvatarSet` / `AvatarAck` / **`AvatarList`**: the relay's datagram naming every member's emoji by `ConnId`, sent only to connections that said Hello; `protocol-spec.md` §9.2). A relay or peer without it just shows the initial. `widgets/emoji.rs`, `features/website.md` "Emoji avatars".
- **PIP spot** — one of the eight places a webcam in picture snaps to when released (four corners and four side middles); `free` only while it is being dragged. `ui/stage/geom.rs` `snap_pip`, `pipinfo ... spot=C,R` in the rig's dump.
- **Playout buffer** (jitter buffer, row 136, 2026-11-04) — the viewer-side delay, per watched stream, that holds still-encoded access units before the decoder so a clump of late frames (a keyframe's transfer over the sharer's uplink) reaches the screen evenly. Dynamic: it grows to the worst recent lateness (`arrival - pts` above the path's floor, 8 s window), shrinks at the **catch-up speed** (5-10 % faster than live), never drops a frame, is capped by Settings > Playback > Jitter buffer (0 = off, default 2 s). Native `decode/playout.rs`, browser `docs/js/core/jitter.js` (same shape, different target). The audio is delayed by the same amount. `features/video-pipeline.md` "Playout buffer".
- **Wanted stream** — a watch carried over a reconnect, identified by owner display name + kind + label (never by `ConnId`/`StreamId`, which change). `session::WantedStream`.

- **Join link** — `server|room-id|key[|certhash[|name]]`; everything a client needs to enter a room. The optional 5th field is the room's display name, percent-encoded UTF-8 (a suggestion that pre-fills the add-room dialog, not part of the room's identity); an empty 4th field means no certhash. `protocol-spec.md` §11.4.
- **Cert hash** (`certhash`) — SHA-256 of the relay's leaf TLS certificate (DER), base64url without padding; what a browser pins via WebTransport `serverCertificateHashes`. Changes whenever the relay rotates its self-signed cert. `protocol-spec.md` §11.3.
- **vbox / hbox / slot / fill** — the egui UI's layout model (`client/src/widgets/layout.rs`, module doc of `widgets/mod.rs`). A **vbox** is a vertical container (`ui.vertical`, or a card/well frame that only paints and pads); an **hbox** is a row built with `HBox`; a **slot** is a fixed-width cell of an hbox (`fixed(w)`); **fill** is the cell that takes the remaining width (`FILL`; several fills share it equally). egui has no flexbox, so `HBox` does explicit width math. The browser UI mirrors the names (`V`/`H`/`SL`/`FILL`). `ui/STYLING-GUIDE.md`, `features/website.md`.
- **GUI lock** (historical) — the `flock` on `/tmp/claude-1000/sss-gui.lock` (fd 9) that agents held around an interactive GUI session on the user's real shared desktop (launch, focus, `ydotool`, `grim`). Used until 2026-10-14; the headless rig is private per worktree and needs no lock. Only legacy scripts and the `SSS_GUI_LOCKED=1` opt-in name of the browser harness (`docs/dev/README.md`) still mention it. `claude-instructions/testing-and-verification.md` "History".
- **Headless rig** (`hs-rig.sh`) — the private headless sway test environment (`client/scripts/hs-rig.sh up|status|run|down`, driven with `hs-drive.sh`/`hs-scenario.sh`): virtual outputs, its own dbus/PipeWire/portal/relay/two clients, all under one private `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`. All real-app tests run in it since 2026-10-14 (user request: stay off their desktop); every worktree has its own. `claude-instructions/testing-and-verification.md` "The rig", `client/scripts/README.md`.
- **Captured output** (`HEADLESS-1`) — the rig's 1920x1080@240 Hz virtual output the portal shares, kept busy by a fullscreen `vkcube` (the test pattern); `HEADLESS-2` hosts the sharer client, `HEADLESS-3` the viewer client. `client/scripts/README.md`.
- **Legacy (real-desktop) script** — a script that acts on the user's real desktop session (Hyprland/`hyprctl`, or MangoWC/`mmsg`; `ydotool`, the real portal picker). Carries a `LEGACY (2026-10-14)` banner and, for the repo-root `launch-test*.sh` and `docs/dev/e2e/native-session.sh`, refuses without `SSS_ALLOW_REAL_DESKTOP=1` (the user's own manual use). Agents never run them. `client/scripts/README.md` "Legacy".
- **Gallery** (`--gallery`) — `simple-screensharing-client --gallery`: a screen that renders every widget helper with fake data (`client/src/widgets/gallery.rs`). Right for widget and token work, never proof that a screen or action works. `claude-instructions/testing-and-verification.md`, `ui/STYLING-GUIDE.md`.
- **Sidecar** (historical) — the Rust/Tauri process the old Electron shell spawned (`client/src-tauri`, with `video_server.rs`). Gone with the egui rebuild (deleted in the egui rework (I23)); old docs still say "sidecar". `features/video-pipeline.md` "History", `architecture/overview.md`.
- **Sniffing** — detecting a stream's video codec (H.264 / H.265 / AV1) from its bitstream instead of a tag on the wire: the first byte separates Annex-B from an AV1 OBU stream, and H.264/H.265 parameter-set NAL headers are disjoint. Native `decode/codec.rs::sniff`; browser `docs/js/core/codec.js`. `features/video-pipeline.md` "Native decode", `architecture/protocol-spec.md` §6.4.
- **Capture domain** — which memory the capture tee carries (`capture.rs` `CaptureDomain`, #39): **GPU** (Linux portal on a working VA stack: `pipewiresrc` DMA-BUF to `vapostproc` to VAMemory NV12, no CPU copy) or **System** (original CPU shape: Windows, software `x264enc`, tests). `features/stream-control.md` "Capture/encode performance".
- **RateCap** — `capture::branch::RateCap`: the per-destination fps limiter on a branch's valve. The configured fps is a cap, never a floor; nothing ever duplicates a frame. `features/stream-control.md` "Capture/encode performance".
- **stableKey** (browser) — a stream's identity across reconnects: room + sharer name + kind + label (a reconnect changes ConnId and so the stream key). The UI's 60 s resume list is keyed by it; the native equivalent is **Wanted stream**. `features/website.md`, `docs/js/core/README.md`.
- **Live preview** (own-tile preview, #78) — the sharer's own tile on the **Stage** (before the stage: a card in "Sharing in this room") showing a live <= 1920x1080 / <= 30 fps picture of what they capture, only while the window is focused (native `capture.rs` `LivePreview`, drawn by `ui/stage/tile.rs` `mine_tile`, valve-gated tee branch; browser `capture.js` `previewFor`, a muted `<video>`). Not the ~30 s JPEG **room-preview thumbnails** other members see (`preview.rs`). `features/self-preview.md`.
- **On air vs starting** (UI) — a share's phase in a room (`ui::MyPhase`): **on air** = at least one destination there is Live, Rebuilding or Stalled; **starting** = none is on air yet but one is still Starting (e.g. waiting for the portal's first frame). Drives the sidebar cast icon and the "Live in N rooms" line. `ui/APP-SHELL.md`, `features/stream-control.md` "Idle monitors".
