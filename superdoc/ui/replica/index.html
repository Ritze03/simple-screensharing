Replica of the native egui client with fake data. Stage is the proposed room view (#134), and Watched the same stage with everything you watch from every room (#136): drag a stream to reorder it (Split, Crop) or move it (Floating), drag the gaps between streams to resize, right-click a stream or a room for its menu, hover an avatar for stream stats, Settings > Profile sets an emoji avatar, the arrows in the bottom bar open the share, audio, webcam and layout menus, and the layout menu's "Webcam in picture" puts a webcam inside its owner's screen share (drag it, resize it by its corner handle). Classic is the current app. Double-click a stream for fullscreen; Esc leaves. Colours, type and sizes are the CSS tokens at the top of the file, named after client/src/theme.rs.
